    How the risk level is set

    Each order row gets one warning sign for each of these, from public sources only:

    • Not a firm order: provisional, letter of intent, "up to", or partly provisional terms.
    • Changed owner since ordering: the customer merged or was bought after it signed.
    • Parent trimmed other aircraft orders in 2026.
    • Ordered over 3 years ago, before Nov 2023, with deliveries still ahead.

    None of these means an order is cancelled. They mark where REGENT may need to reconfirm.
